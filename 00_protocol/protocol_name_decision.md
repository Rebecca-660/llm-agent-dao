# Final Protocol Name Decision

## Decision

- Final experimental name: `Kelmoryn Protocol`
- Decision date: 2026-08-26
- Decision authority: Researcher confirmation
- Status: Selected for consistent use in subsequent prompt rendering and experimental materials

## Basis for inclusion

The researcher selected `Kelmoryn Protocol` after reviewing the existing naming evidence. The existing external-search record marks `Kelmoryn Protocol` as a passing candidate, and the archived screenshot records the corresponding search. The existing model-memory diagnostic reports that OpenAI Codex did not recognize the exact name as a specific established blockchain, cryptocurrency, DeFi protocol, DAO, or other well-known real-world project.

This decision relies on the following existing evidence without altering or replacing it:

- `00_protocol/protocol_name_check.md`
- `00_protocol/0B.2搜索截图/Kelmoryn.png`
- `00_protocol/leakage_model_checks.jsonl`

## Alternatives not selected

- `Selyth Protocol` also remained a passing candidate in the existing external-search and model-diagnostic records, but the researcher selected one name to keep protocol identity constant across experimental conditions.
- `Caldris Protocol`, `Veyridian DAO`, `Orivane Network`, `Arqadia DAO`, and `Drovaryn DAO` were not selected because the existing external-check record notes a real-world correspondence or a similar name.

The alternatives remain part of the provenance record and are not deleted or rewritten.

## Evidence boundary

The archived search and model-memory checks are screening evidence for experimental-name leakage. They do not establish that no current or future project uses a similar name. The model diagnostic is not an external registry search, and the available record covers OpenAI Codex rather than every model family that may later be used.

This decision is not a trademark search, legal clearance, or representation that the name is available for commercial use. The name is selected solely as a fictional identifier for this controlled research experiment.
