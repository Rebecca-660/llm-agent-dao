# Persona Dimensions v1.0

## Scope

The persona design retains only the dimensions most relevant to long-term capital commitment. All other financial conditions are fixed.

## Varying Dimensions

| Dimension | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Holding tenure | 6 months | 2 years |
| Portfolio exposure | 10% of crypto portfolio | 50% of crypto portfolio |
| Governance involvement | Passive holder | Active voter |
| Prior major crypto incident experience | No prior experience | Previously experienced one |

The full factorial design contains `2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16` personas.

## Portfolio Exposure Amounts

- 10% exposure corresponds to a total crypto portfolio of $80,000.
- 50% exposure corresponds to a total crypto portfolio of $16,000.

## Fixed Conditions

- The current staked governance-token position is worth $8,000.
- 80% of the governance tokens held on this platform are currently staked.
- The staking APY is 6%.
- Unstaking requires a 7-day waiting period and carries no additional penalty.
- The broader crypto market has not experienced significant movement that day.
- The investor's personal liquidity needs have not changed.
- The incident has just occurred, and the governance-token price has not yet changed materially.

## Retail Investor Capability Anchor

> You are a retail cryptocurrency investor, not a professional trader,
> smart-contract developer, or blockchain security researcher.
